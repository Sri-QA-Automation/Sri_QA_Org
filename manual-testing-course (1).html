Manual Testing: frequently asked questions

Can a fresher join the manual testing course?

Yes. The course starts from the very basics and needs no prior IT experience.

Is manual testing still in demand?

Yes. Manual testing skills such as test design and bug reporting are needed even in automation roles, and they are the best starting point.

Will I learn JIRA?

Yes. You practise raising and tracking bugs in JIRA during the live project.

What should I learn after manual testing?

Most learners continue to automation with Selenium or Playwright, and then to AI testing.

Are online batches available?

Yes. We run online and offline batches.
